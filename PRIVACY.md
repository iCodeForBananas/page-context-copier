# Privacy Policy — Page Context Copier

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Page Context Copier does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **What it reads:** The extension reads the content of a web page (text, structure, accessibility attributes and metadata) only when you click its toolbar button, press its keyboard shortcut, or choose its context-menu item. Access is limited to the tab you're using at that moment.
- **Where the data goes:** The result is written to your device's clipboard. Nothing is sent to the developer or any other server. The extension makes no network requests.
- **What it stores:** Nothing. There is no storage, no analytics, no tracking and no account.
- **Sensitive fields:** Values of password, credit-card and one-time-code fields are replaced with "(filled, value hidden)", and hidden form fields are skipped.

Anything you paste somewhere else after copying is covered by the privacy policy of the service you paste it into.

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
